Biography

About

Elsayed Amer Hassan is a petroleum and natural gas engineer with more than fourteen years in upstream oil and gas across Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Egypt. He is Operations Manager for Gas Compression Services at Petrogistix Petroleum & Energy Logistics and Services Co. in Al Khobar, where he leads a team of about twenty senior engineers, engineers, department managers, technicians and operators delivering wellhead compression, well services and early production facilities for Saudi Aramco.

His career runs through the whole production chain. He began as a field engineer with Weatherford Drilling International on rigs in Egypt's Western Desert, moved into natural gas processing with Suez Oil Company (SUCO), where he worked on gas receiving, separation, glycol dehydration, refrigeration and low-temperature separation, and then into well intervention on gas and condensate wells, leading coiled tubing, nitrogen lifting, acid stimulation, wireline and recompletion work. His last position in the Egyptian petroleum sector was Production Process Section Head in the General Operations Administration.

At Petrogistix he moved from senior petroleum engineer to operations manager, first for ADNOC onshore wellhead services in Abu Dhabi and then for gas compression services in Saudi Arabia. His teams engineered and commissioned Saudi Aramco's first ultra-low-pressure wellhead compression system at Haradh GOSP-4, a sour gas well flowing below 25 psig, recovering 5 MMSCFD of gas and 500 barrels per day of condensate. For ADNOC they restored more than fifteen wells to 4,000 barrels of oil per day and 3 MMSCFD through zero-flaring separation, mobile compression and reinforced thermoplastic pipe export.

Alongside operations, Elsayed has worked as an independent oil and gas training consultant since 2015 and has delivered more than 2,000 hours of technical training in Egypt, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Oman and Turkey. He taught natural gas processing at Al-Azhar University, has led workshops for SPE and IADC student chapters, and writes about what he builds: four conference papers at GOTECH and URTeC between 2025 and 2026 on liquid loading, surface jet pumps, mobile wellhead compression and machine learning for compressor performance.

He is a registered engineer with the Saudi Council of Engineers, a member of the Society of Petroleum Engineers, and is completing an MBA at UTAMED in Spain and CMI Level 7 studies in strategic management.
